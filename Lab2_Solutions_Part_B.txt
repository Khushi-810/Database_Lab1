# **Part B – Relational Tables**

---

### **Department**

* department_id (PK)
* department_name
* office_location

---

### **Student**

* student_id (PK)
* name
* date_of_birth
* gender
* contact_number
* department_id (FK)

---

### **Faculty**

* faculty_id (PK)
* name
* designation
* email
* department_id (FK)

---

### **Course**

* course_id (PK)
* course_name
* credits
* department_id (FK)
* faculty_id (FK)

---

### **Enrollment**

* student_id (PK, FK)
* course_id (PK, FK)
* semester
* grade

(Composite Primary Key → **student_id + course_id**)
